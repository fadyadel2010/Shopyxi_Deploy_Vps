## Authority and delivery contract

- SQL order, attribution, tenant, affiliate, promotion link, and immutable server-issued click/product correlation remain authoritative.
- Checkout persists `AffiliateOrderPlacedEvent` in the SQL outbox inside its transaction. The consumer re-reads committed SQL; broker unavailability does not roll back a successfully committed COD order.
- A conversion is a **committed attributed order**, timestamped from persisted order creation, not processing time, payment, delivery, commission, or settlement.
- Mongo `affiliate_order_conversions` is insert-only by order ID. Exact click updates require matching tenant/affiliate/referral/product identity and compatible existing conversion state. Failures propagate to the broker retry/error path.
- No “latest unconverted click” guessing remains. Historical orders without trustworthy correlation can receive an order projection, but ambiguous clicks remain unlinked.
- The dashboard distinguishes request-level tracked click events from persisted click records. Rate is attributed orders / click records × 100 for selected UTC days; an unavailable/zero denominator is not fabricated as a measured zero.
