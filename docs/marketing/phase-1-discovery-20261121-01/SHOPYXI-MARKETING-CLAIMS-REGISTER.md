# SHOPYXI — Marketing claims register

**Phase 1 · 2026-11-21 · all wording is proposed, none is publication-authorized.**

## 1. Controls

This register controls the exact factual/editorial copy in the bilingual messaging foundation. Each row contains two separately reviewable language versions: **EN** and **AR**. Evidence IDs resolve to named paths/symbols/reports in [the feature matrix](SHOPYXI-FEATURE-EVIDENCE-MATRIX.md).

Verification status describes evidence, not publication approval:

- **VERIFIED** = bounded current-source capability with corroboration.
- **PARTIAL** = implemented but material scope/acceptance conditions remain.
- **PROPOSED** = positioning/benefit/CTA, not an independently proven business outcome.
- **OWNER FACT** = requires commercial, legal or process confirmation.

Use the five feature statuses in the feature matrix for product availability; editorial statuses here do not upgrade them. **Approval status of every row: PENDING.** No rate, threshold, response time, image, quotation or metric may be substituted without review. All translations require Arabic editorial approval.

Approval roles: **PO** product/commercial owner; **RO** release owner; **FO** relevant finance/commission owner; **AO** application/access owner; **IO** integration owner; **SO** sales operations; **LO** legal/privacy/company owner; **AR** Arabic editor. These are proposed accountable roles, not named people or approvals already obtained.

**Phase 1F-C additions (2026-11-24):** `C27a` records the approved proposed first sentence of `C27`; `C50` and `C51` record the Capabilities & Coverage section headline and subtitle. All three are **PROPOSED** fragments and their approval state is **PENDING**. The original `C27` row is unchanged.

**Phase 1F-D additions (2026-11-25):** `C52`, `C53`, `C54` and `C55` record the Free Experience CTA headline, sub-headline, description and primary CTA label. All four are **PROPOSED** and their approval state is **PENDING**. `C39`, `C41`, `C42` and `C49` are unchanged.
